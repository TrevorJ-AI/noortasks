Use on my phone

Your computer is your NoorTasks hub. Keep it awake and NoorTasks running for your phone to sync.

Create your account in NoorTasks first, then return here to pair your phone.

Connect with Tailscale

Private HTTPS lets your phone sync away from home and install the web app. Install Tailscale on both devices and sign in to the same tailnet.

Checking Tailscale…

Uses HTTPS port 8443. The Tailscale proxy stays configured after you quit NoorTasks. To remove it, run tailscale serve --https=8443 off on this computer.

On the same Wi-Fi

Local network access uses unencrypted HTTP. Use it only on a trusted home network. It cannot provide web app installation, service workers or Web Push on a phone. Allow NoorTasks through your firewall and never forward this port on your router.

Pair your phone

For Wi-Fi, choose this computer’s address on your home network. Addresses can change when the computer reconnects.

Scan in the NoorTasks Android app, or open the link in your phone’s browser. Each code works once and expires after 10 minutes. Treat it like a password.
